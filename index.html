01 / The apps

Three apps for the things you already own.

A remote, a notebook and a server console. Each one does a single job, and the launcher keeps them together.

Your TV remote, on the phone already in your hand. It finds the TVs on your Wi-Fi, pairs once and remembers them, with a full keyboard for searches and no hub or extra hardware.

02 / Works with

It fits the tools you already use.

No new account and no parallel ecosystem. Each app connects to what you already have: your TV, Siri, your assistant, your devices, your server.

Siri

By voice, without opening the app

Turn the TV on or off, mute it, change the volume or control playback. Create, append to and find notes. Ask the app to refresh when you want the latest picture.

AI

Your assistant, your rules

Your AI assistant can read and act on your notes, server and saved TVs. Each app shares only what you allow, and only while you ask it to.

Sync

Your data stays yours

Notes can sync between your devices. The server shows what it runs and what it is downloading. Nothing passes through an IgelTools account.

Setup, supported products and current limits are in the IgelTools Docs ↗.

03 / What to expect

Local, calm, honest.

Six habits shared by every app, whichever part of your setup it talks to.

01

Local first

Your TVs, your server and your notes stay on your side. Nothing takes a detour through a relay of ours.

02

No IgelTools account

Nothing to register with us. The apps work with the Wi-Fi, accounts and assistant you already have.

03

One job per app

Each app solves one problem completely instead of half-solving four. The menu stays short on purpose.

04

Connected, not locked in

The apps work with the products you already own and the services you already run. There is no IgelTools cloud to migrate to.

05

Honest about limits

Fire TV support is limited, Apple TV is not supported, and some features are still growing. The apps say so instead of pretending.

06

Built to last

An app that ships keeps getting fixes. Nothing here is a weekend experiment left to rot.

05 / In use

A small app for the moment you need it.

None of these tools asks you to change how you live. They fit into the parts of the day where a bigger app would be too much.

Home

In the living room

Find the TV, pair once, then change the volume, search for a film or wake it from standby without hunting for the remote.

Notes

At the desk or on the sofa

Write a note, sketch a diagram, ask KIgel for a hand, then find it again on another device.

Server

When the server needs you

Check what the machine is doing, restart a service, look at an app or the download queue, then put the phone away.

Setup, requirements and the details behind each feature are in the IgelTools Docs ↗.
