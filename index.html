02 / How it is built

Local, native, honest.

Six habits shared by every app here, whichever machine it talks to.

01

Local first

The TVs are on your network, the server is a box you own, the notes sit in your own iCloud. Nothing takes a detour through a relay of ours.

02

No IgelTools account

Nothing to register. The apps work with the machines and accounts you already have: your Wi-Fi, your SSH key, your iCloud.

03

Native, not wrapped

SwiftUI on Apple platforms, Rust and Tauri on Linux. No web view pretending to be an app.

04

One job per app

Each app solves one problem completely instead of half-solving four. The menu stays short on purpose.

05

Honest about limits

Fire TV is marked Limited, Apple TV is out of scope, the interactive terminal is not wired up yet. The apps say so instead of pretending.

06

Built to last

An app that ships keeps getting fixes. Nothing here is a weekend experiment left to rot.

03 / Supported TVs

Nine platforms, one remote.

Inside Igel TVRemote, support follows the protocol the TV speaks, not the logo on the box. If it is on your network and speaks one of these, the app can drive it.

PanasonicVIERA
Fire TVLimited · ADB

Fire TV is marked Limited: current Fire OS no longer lets third-party apps control the TV locally, so it needs developer options and ADB debugging switched on. Apple TV and infrared-only TVs are not supported.
