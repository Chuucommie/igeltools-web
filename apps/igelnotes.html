IgelNotes

A notebook for pages that hold more than text. Write in blocks, sketch with the Pencil, find everything later, and let KIgel help with the page when you want a hand.

01 / What it does

Pages made of blocks, not one long text field.

Every note is a stack of independent blocks, so a page can hold prose, a table, a sketch and a code snippet without turning into a mess.

01 · Blocks

Every kind of block

Twenty-one block types: headings, text, to-dos, toggles, quotes, callouts, tables, code, drawings, images, sub-pages, media and web bookmarks. Reorder them without rewriting anything.

02 · Organise

Find it later

Workspaces, favourites, tags, templates and sub-pages keep a growing notebook navigable. The trash holds deleted notes before they go for good.

03 · Pencil

Draw in the middle of a page

A drawing canvas with its own toolbar, kept as a block next to the text — for diagrams, margin scribbles and handwritten lists.

04 · Siri

Hands-off capture

Create a note, append to one, search your notes or open a page by name from Siri or a Shortcut. There is a shopping-list shortcut too.

05 · Assistant

KIgel, and the assistants you use

KIgel works with the AI you choose. On the Mac, you can also let an assistant read or edit notes, with permissions you choose.

06 · Sync

Your notes, your service

Notes stay on your device and can sync between your devices through the service you choose. Nothing is parked in an IgelTools account.

Documented. How to set up KIgel, sync and the Siri shortcuts is in the IgelTools Docs ↗.
