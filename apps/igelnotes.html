IgelNotes

A block editor in the Notion mould, built on SwiftUI, SwiftData and CloudKit. Write in blocks, sketch with the Pencil, and let an assistant work on the page with you.

01 / What it does

Pages made of blocks, not one long text field.

Every note is a stack of independent blocks, so a page can hold prose, a table, a sketch and a code snippet without turning into a mess.

01 · Blocks

Every kind of block

Text, headings, quotes, callouts, to-dos, tables, code, images, drawings and web bookmarks. Reorder them without rewriting anything.

02 · Sync

Sync you do not manage

Notes are stored with SwiftData and carried between devices by CloudKit, so the page you started on the phone opens on the Mac.

03 · Pencil

Draw in the middle of a page

A drawing canvas with its own toolbar, kept as a block next to the text — for diagrams, margin scribbles and handwritten lists.

04 · Siri

Hands-off capture

App Intents create a note, append to one, search your notes or open a page by name, from Siri or a Shortcut. There is a shopping-list intent too.

05 · Assistant

KIgel, on your terms

An assistant that can read and act on notes, using the provider you choose — Apple Intelligence, Ollama on your own machine, OpenAI or Anthropic.

06 · Sharing

Out of the app when you want

Share a note or export it as a payload, including to an external agent over HTTP when you want one to take the page further.

Agent-ready and documented. Protocols, security and the MCP tool reference live in the IgelTools Docs ↗.
