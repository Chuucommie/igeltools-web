IgelLauncher

One window on your Mac to install, update and open the other three. It picks the right version for your machine, checks every download and keeps the suite current without a browser.

01 / What it does

The suite, in one window.

The same idea as the apps themselves: one clear job. The launcher reads the suite catalog, finds what is already installed, and only offers versions that match your Mac.

01 · Catalog

Every app in one list

Igel TVRemote, IgelNotes and IgelServer with their descriptions and status. Search the suite, filter to what is installed, and see which copies came from the launcher.

02 · Installs

The right version, first time

The right version chosen for your machine. No hunting through release pages.

03 · Verified

Nothing unchecked

Every download is checked before it opens. A bad file is deleted and the install stops.

04 · Updates

Keeps the suite current

Checks each app's releases, compares versions and offers one update or all of them, with a progress bar and a cancel button. Pre-releases are opt-in.

05 · Quiet

Quiet, and connected

No browser, no account and no tracking. Claude Desktop, Cursor and Zed can list the suite, check updates or open an app, using the same checks as the window.

06 · Languages

English, Italiano, Deutsch

Follows the system language by default, switches immediately in Settings, and remembers the choice between runs.

Documented. Setup and current limits are in the IgelTools Docs ↗.
