Igel TVRemote

Remote for smart TVs on Wi-Fi. Pair once, then navigate, type and control the volume — no hub, no IR blaster, no account.

01 / What it does

Everything the remote in the drawer does — plus a keyboard.

The app talks to the TV over your own network, so it works from the kitchen, from the sofa, or from wherever the other remote ended up.

01 · Pairing

Pair once, then forget it

It finds the TVs on your network, pairs once and remembers them. Afterwards it reconnects by itself, even when the TV gets a new address from DHCP.

02 · Keys

The complete key set

D-pad, OK, back, home, menu, volume, mute, channel, playback, number pad and colour keys wherever the platform supports them.

03 · Typing

Type instead of tapping

Send text from the phone or computer keyboard, so searching the TV stops being a walk across an on-screen alphabet.

04 · Watch

On your wrist

Tap or tilt to navigate, use the Digital Crown for volume, and switch to the keypad or media screen without picking up the phone.

05 · Discovery

Built for real networks

SSDP, Bonjour and a subnet sweep run in parallel. If the router filters multicast, any protocol still accepts an address typed by hand.

06 · Standby

Wake it back up

A wake-on-LAN button for TVs that keep their network interface alive in standby — offered only where the address is known, and never on iPhone.

Nine platforms on your Wi-Fi.

Fire TVLimited · ADB

Fire TV is marked Limited: recent Fire OS versions removed the local service third-party apps used to pair, so control falls back to ADB and needs developer options enabled on the TV. Apple TV is not supported, and neither is anything that only listens for infrared.

03 / Details

Requirements and habits.

Platforms
iPhone (iOS 17+), Mac (macOS 13+), Apple Watch (watchOS 10+, through a paired iPhone)
Network
The TV and this device have to share a Wi-Fi network. Guest networks and VLANs isolate clients even when the name looks the same.
Storage
Pairing credentials live in the Apple Keychain, keyed to the TV and the client identity rather than to an IP address. TV names and preferences stay on the device.
Privacy
No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Requests go to the TVs on your network and nowhere else.
Not supported
Apple TV, infrared-only TVs, and TVs that are not reachable on the same network.
Languages
English and Italian, from a single shared string catalog so the three apps cannot drift apart.
Status
In development. Builds run on physical devices; the App Store listing is being prepared.

Not released yet. The iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch versions build from the repository today. Windows, Android and Linux come after 1.0.
