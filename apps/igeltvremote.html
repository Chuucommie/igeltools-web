Igel TVRemote

Your TV remote, on the phone already in your hand. Pair once, then navigate, type, change the volume and wake the TV from standby — no hub, no extra hardware, no account.

01 / What it does

Everything the remote in the drawer does — plus a keyboard.

The app talks to the TV on your own Wi-Fi, so it works from the kitchen, from the sofa, or from wherever the other remote ended up.

01 · Pairing

Pair once, then forget it

It finds the TVs on your Wi-Fi, pairs once and remembers them. It reconnects by itself, even when the TV changes address, and wakes a TV from standby where the TV supports it.

02 · Keys

The complete key set

Direction buttons, OK, back, home, menu, volume, mute, channel, playback, number pad and colour keys wherever the TV supports them.

03 · Typing

Type instead of tapping

Send text from the phone or computer keyboard, so searching the TV stops being a walk across an on-screen alphabet.

04 · Watch

On your wrist

Tap or tilt to navigate, use the Digital Crown for volume, and switch to the keypad or media screen without picking up the phone.

05 · TVs

The TVs you already own

Samsung, LG, Roku, Google TV, Vizio, Philips, Sony and Panasonic are supported. Fire TV works with limits; Apple TV is not supported.

06 · Siri and voice

Control it without the app

Siri and Shortcuts turn the TV on or off, mute it, change the volume or control playback, without opening the app.

Documented. Which controls each TV supports and what Fire TV needs is in the IgelTools Docs ↗.
